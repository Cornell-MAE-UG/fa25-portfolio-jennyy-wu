## Project Overview

SALEM (Semi-Autonomous Lightweight Extinguishing Machine) was our team's response to a simulated building fire. We built a system that could detect a light marking a fire, aim a foam-dart launcher, and fire at the target. The operator selected the floor and reloaded the launcher.

We combined a 3D-printed structure, horizontal and vertical servos, a light sensor, and the provided launcher. After testing the parts and the full assembly, we adjusted the weight distribution with a clamp and counterweight. The design had to fit within a 16-inch cube, weigh less than 7 pounds, and stay under a $100 budget.

Our team earned first place in the Sikorsky STEM Challenge.
