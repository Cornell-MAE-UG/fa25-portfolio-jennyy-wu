## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University with a passion for robotics, mechanical design, and bringing ideas to life.

Through Cornell Cup Robotics, I've worked on everything from gear-driven mechanisms that recreate the movement of the International Space Station to underwater robots and autonomous systems. I especially enjoy the process of taking an idea from CAD to a physical prototype, troubleshooting what doesn't work, and improving it until it does.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy golfing, painting, and baking.
